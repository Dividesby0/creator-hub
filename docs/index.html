Plan it. Post it. See what worked.

Creator Hub is a desktop app for Mac and Windows that schedules and publishes your posts to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Facebook and X, and shows all your numbers in one place.

Write once, post everywhere

Compose a post, tailor captions per platform, approve it, and Creator Hub publishes it on time.

Every number together

YouTube Analytics, Google Analytics, Search Console, Business Profile and your social stats in one dashboard, with PDF reports.

Private by design

Your content and sign-ins stay encrypted on your own computer. We never receive your posts or platform data.

How Creator Hub uses Google data

When you choose Connect with Google, Creator Hub asks for permission to upload the YouTube videos you schedule and to read your YouTube Analytics, Google Analytics, Search Console and Business Profile data. That data is used only to show your statistics inside the app on your computer. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Creator Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full privacy policy.

You can disconnect at any time in Creator Hub, or remove access from your Google account permissions.
