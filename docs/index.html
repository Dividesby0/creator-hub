First open on a Mac
  1. Open the download and drag Spektly into Applications.
  2. Open Spektly. If macOS says it can't check it, open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway.
First open on Windows
  1. Run Spektly-Setup.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

How Spektly uses Google data

When you choose Connect with Google, Spektly asks for permission to upload the YouTube videos you schedule and to read your YouTube Analytics, Google Analytics, Search Console and Business Profile data. That data is used only to show your statistics inside the app on your computer. It is never sold, shared or used for advertising. Spektly's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full privacy policy.

You can disconnect at any time in Spektly, or remove access from your Google account permissions.
