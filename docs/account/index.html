07Account

Your Spektly account.

Bought a plan as a guest? Add your email here. Your account keeps every key you buy in one place, lets us send plan and billing updates to the app, and enters you in the monthly customer giveaways if you want in.

We use your email only for Spektly. See the privacy policy.

Billing, receipts and cancelling

Your purchase email has a link to manage your plan: change it, update your card, download receipts or cancel. Lost it? Email info@spektly.com from the address you paid with.

Moving to a new computer

In Spektly, open Settings, then License, and click Deactivate this computer. Then paste your key on the new one.
